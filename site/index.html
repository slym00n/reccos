Every recco starts as Loved it, It was fine or Didn’t like it, and a couple of quick head‑to‑heads place it among the others in your list. That gives it a score out of 10: 8 and up is loved, 5 to 7.9 is fine, and under 5 you didn’t like it. A place’s Locals score brings together everyone who’s recommended it.

Your reccos sit on your profile, so anyone you send your link to can open your list, and people can follow you in the app. The Privacy Policy has the details.
